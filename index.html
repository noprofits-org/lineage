        How to read Lineage

        Edges encode citation, from the citing paper to the cited paper — usually, but not always, newer to older. Read the graph left-to-right as intellectual influence.

        Search and paper metadata come from Crossref. Known open citation links come from OpenCitations; coverage and counts may differ between the two sources.

        Drag papers up or down to arrange a crowded year. The node-spacing control changes the automatic separation; reflow restores the automatic vertical layout.
